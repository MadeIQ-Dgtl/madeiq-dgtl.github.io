Frequently asked questions

Is this a subscription?

No. The listed price is €19.90 once for a digital download. Check the final price and applicable taxes on Payhip before paying.

What will I download?

A ZIP containing the offline web application, quick-start instructions and a customer-use licence. Extract the ZIP before opening index.html.

How do I create a PDF?

Use Print / Save PDF in the tool, then choose the PDF option available in your browser or device print dialog.

Can I move a quote to another device?

Export a JSON backup and import it on the other device. Local saving stays in the browser where you saved it; there is no automatic cloud sync.

Does it set my prices or send quotes automatically?

No. You enter your quantities, prices, markup, discount and tax rate. Review the completed quote before sharing it with your customer.
